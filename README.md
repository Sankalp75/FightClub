# FightClub

> **Learn. Build. Fail. Iterate. Share.**

FightClub is a community of people who want to go beyond conventional engineering.

We believe engineering is not just about **CGPA, certificates, or completing a syllabus**. It is about learning, building, solving real problems, and turning ideas into reality.

We are building a community for people who are **willing to put in the work**.

---

## What We Do

We learn by building real projects across areas such as:

* 🤖 Robotics & Autonomous Systems
* 🚁 UAVs & Drones
* ⚙️ Automation & Embedded Systems
* 💻 Software & AI
* 👁️ Computer Vision
* 🔩 Mechanical Design & Fabrication
* 🔬 Research & Experimentation

Our approach is simple:

**Learn → Build → Fail → Improve → Document**

---

## A Platform for Individuals

FightClub is not only about working as a team.

It is also a platform where individuals can:

* Learn new skills
* Work on real projects
* Collaborate with others
* Take responsibility
* Experiment with their ideas
* Document their work
* Build their GitHub portfolio
* Showcase what they can actually do

We want members to build **proof of work**, not just collect certificates.

> **Don't just say you know something. Build something that proves it.**

---

## Our Philosophy

We are not against academics.

We simply believe that **CGPA is not the complete measure of an engineer.**

We want to create an environment where curiosity, consistency, problem-solving, teamwork, and the willingness to build matter just as much.

We don't want to just learn how things work.

**We want to make them work.**

---

## Community

If you have an idea you want to build, something you want to learn, or simply the determination to work and grow — **FightClub is for you.**

Learn from each other.
Build together.
Help each other grow.

### **Build your skills. Build your projects. Build yourself.**
